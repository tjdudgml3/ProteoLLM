Following publication of the original article [

], the authors reported that the Data Availability section was incorrectly revised following the proofing stage by the Production team. The correct section is as follows:
Metagenomic and metatranscriptomic data are available on SRA, under the BioProject: PRJNA782492. The mass spectrometry proteomics data have been deposited to the ProteomeXchange Consortium via the PRIDE partner repository with the dataset identifier PXD031457. The IMP pipeline, which was used for analysis of metagenomic and metatranscriptomic data, is available at
https://gitlab.lcsb.uni.lu/IMP/imp3
. The R and python code used for statistical analyses and visualizations is available at
https://gitlab.com/uniluxembourg/lcsb/systems-ecology/multi_omics_of_pd_and_irbd
. Metadata are available via the ELIXIR Luxembourg Data Catalogue:
https://datacatalog.elixir-luxembourg.org/e/dataset/ELU-2-81D473-1
.
The original article has been updated.
